Your Market Moment

Here is what we found.

Two forces open these conversations, and neither is compliance. German manufacturers are under pressure to show progress on AI, which starts with getting data out of on-premises systems and into somewhere it can actually be used. Separately, funded storage refreshes and migration programmes come round on their own cycle. Road.MAP sits exactly where those two meet — moving the data onto AWS via NetApp ONTAP, without moving or rebuilding everything else.

AI
The strongest current driver — pressure to adopt at almost any cost, which begins with making on-prem data usable in the cloud2
Funded
Concretely planned refreshes, migrations and modernizations — the trigger that has actually opened engagements to date2
4-phase
Road.MAP methodology — assessment, secure target-state design, implementation, optimization1

Storm Reply wins by being handed a problem, over-delivering on it, and expanding from there — which works beautifully once you are inside an account, and not at all for getting into a new one.
