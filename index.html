Your Market Moment

Here is what we found.

Two forces are converging on legacy storage estates: enterprises are moving business-critical data off aging on-prem systems onto AWS, and tightening EU/German security and compliance rules (NIS2, BSI C5, GDPR) make credential protection and auditability a condition of that move. Storm Reply's Road.MAP answers the first; the built-in NetApp and CyberArk stack answers the second.

4-phase
Road.MAP methodology — assessment, secure target-state design, implementation, optimization1
NIS2
EU directive obliging risk management, access control and incident reporting at essential entities2
BSI C5
The German cloud-security baseline regulated buyers increasingly expect evidence against, alongside GDPR3

"Storage is treated as an afterthought in most migrations. Here it's the way in — and the reason security leaders join the conversation early."

Who You Sell To

One target segment, entered two ways. The accounts are largely the same; what changes is which door you knock on first — the infrastructure team, for whom legacy storage is the blocker, or the security office, for whom a compliance deadline is. Named accounts built from your list at kickoff.

Target Segment Automotive & manufacturing enterprises in Germany and EMEA
1,000+ employees, €100M+ revenue, multi-site IT estates. Business-critical applications still anchored to on-premises storage, an AWS migration either running or stalled, and a regulatory floor — NIS2, BSI C5, GDPR — that makes how the data moves as scrutinised as whether it moves.
